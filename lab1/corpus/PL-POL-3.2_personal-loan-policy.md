# Meridian Finance - Personal Loan Policy (PL-POL-3.2)
Effective: 1 April 2026 | Owner: Retail Credit | Replaces: PL-POL-3.1

## 1. Eligibility and limits
1.1 Applicants must be salaried or self-employed and must not be older than 60 at loan maturity.
1.2 The sanctioned amount ranges from INR 50,000 to INR 2,500,000.
1.3 The tenor ranges from 12 months to 60 months. Tenor is fixed at sanction and is not extended except through a restructuring approved by Credit Risk.
1.4 A minimum bureau score of 700 is required for loans above INR 1,000,000.

## 2. Top-up loans
2.1 A top-up on an existing personal loan may be sanctioned when the borrower has paid at least 12 EMIs and has had no instalment more than 30 days past due (30+ DPD) in the last 12 months.
2.2 The top-up amount may not exceed 50% of the original sanctioned amount, and the combined tenor may not exceed 60 months from the top-up date. Every top-up is underwritten as a fresh application, including a new bureau pull and income verification.
2.3 Exception: accounts that have been restructured, or that are under settlement, are not eligible for a top-up regardless of repayment history.
2.4 Top-up approval authority: up to INR 500,000 at branch level; above that, regional credit head.

## 3. Documentation
3.1 Salaried applicants provide the last three salary slips and six months of bank statements.
3.2 Self-employed applicants provide two years of returns and six months of bank statements.

## 4. Glossary
EMI - equated monthly instalment.
DPD - days past due: the number of calendar days an instalment remains unpaid after its due date.
Restructured - an account whose repayment terms were changed because of borrower hardship.
