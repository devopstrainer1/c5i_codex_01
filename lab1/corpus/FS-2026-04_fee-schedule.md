# Meridian Finance - Fee Schedule FS-2026-04 (personal loans)
Effective: 1 April 2026

| Fee | Amount | Applies |
|---|---|---|
| Processing fee | 1.5% of sanctioned amount, minimum INR 1,000, excluding GST | Deducted from disbursal |
| Late payment fee | 2% per month on the overdue EMI | After the grace period ends |
| Cheque / ECS bounce charge | INR 500 per instance | Each failed presentation |

Foreclosure charge (percent of principal outstanding at closure):

| Months since disbursal | Charge |
|---|---|
| 0 to 12 | 4% |
| 13 to 24 | 3% |
| Over 24 | 2% |

Note 1: The processing fee is waived for customers who hold a Meridian salary account.
Note 2: Salary-account waivers do not apply to the cheque / ECS bounce charge or to the late payment fee.
Note 3: All fees are subject to GST at the prevailing rate.
