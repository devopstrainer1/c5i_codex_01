# Meridian Finance - Home Loan FAQ (customer-facing, 2026)

## Prepayment and foreclosure
Q: Is there a prepayment penalty on a floating-rate home loan?
A: No. Individual borrowers pay no prepayment or foreclosure charge on floating-rate home loans, whether they pay part of the loan or close it in full.

Q: What about fixed-rate home loans?
A: A charge of 2% of the principal prepaid applies to fixed-rate home loans. Part-prepayments up to 25% of the outstanding principal in a financial year are exempt from this charge.

## Tenor and limits
Q: What is the maximum tenor for a home loan?
A: Up to 30 years (360 months), subject to the borrower being no older than 70 at maturity.

Q: What is the minimum home loan amount?
A: INR 300,000. There is no processing fee waiver for home loans.

## Documents
Q: What documents are needed to apply?
A: Identity and address proof, income documents for the last two years, property papers, and the sale agreement.
