# Collections SOP v3.0
Effective: 1 July 2026 | Owner: Collections | Replaces: v2.0

## Grace period and reminders
Personal loan EMIs carry a 3-day grace period before the late payment fee applies. A reminder SMS is sent two days before the due date, on the due date, and on day 2 past due.

## Escalation
Accounts that reach 30 days past due are referred to the field collections team. Accounts at 90 days past due are classified as non-performing. Borrowers who contact the bank before day 30 are offered a repayment plan before any referral.
