# Collections SOP v2.0
Effective: 1 January 2025 | Owner: Collections

## Grace period and reminders
Personal loan EMIs carry a 5-day grace period before the late payment fee applies. A reminder SMS is sent on the due date and again on day 3 past due.

## Escalation
Accounts that reach 30 days past due are referred to the field collections team. Accounts at 90 days past due are classified as non-performing.
